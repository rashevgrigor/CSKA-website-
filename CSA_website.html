Our Story

Built for
Champions

Founded in 1981, CSA CSKA has grown from a small training hall into one of the most respected acrobatic gymnastics clubs in the region. Our philosophy is simple: expert coaching, relentless dedication, and a team culture built on trust between partners.

From children taking their first steps in the sport to athletes competing for the national team, we build champions at every level — on and off the mat.
